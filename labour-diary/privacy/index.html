1.Who we are

Labour Diary (Android package com.aksyssoftware.labourdiary) is an app for labour providers (thekedars) to manage labourers, clients, jobs, daily attendance, wages, advances and settlements. It is developed and operated by AKsys Technologies, Kota, Rajasthan, India ("AKsys", "we", "us").

This Privacy Policy explains what information Labour Diary handles, where it is kept, why, and the choices you have. It is published under the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 with its Rules. By installing or using Labour Diary you agree to this policy.

3.Information we handle

3.1 Information about you

  • Account: your Google account name, email and profile photo, or (in the Several phones mode) a mobile number and password for staff, stored only as a secure hash.
  • Business profile: your business name, phone and the settings you choose.
  • Business records: work categories, jobs, sites, rates, attendance, wages, advances, payments, settlements and reports.

3.2 Information about other people that you enter

Labourers: name, mobile number, village or area, photo, a photo of an ID card (the app does not ask for or store an ID number), skills, rates, attendance, wages, advances and balance. Clients: farmers, builders, households, traders and firms with their contact details, jobs and amounts owed.

For this information you decide what is entered and why, so under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you act as the Data Fiduciary for it. You must have a lawful reason to keep it and should tell those people that you record their details. In the Several phones mode, AKsys only stores and processes it for you as a Data Processor, on your instructions.

3.3 Licence and device information

To check your subscription, the app sends our licence server your account email (or mobile number), the licence ID, the app version and an app-generated device ID. In the Only this phone mode this is the only information that reaches us, and only when the phone is online. We also keep the record of your payment to us (amount, date, mode, and the GST invoice we issue) as tax law requires.

3.4 Information we do not collect

Labour Diary shows no advertisements and contains no advertising or third-party analytics tools. We do not read your SMS, call logs or files outside the app, and we do not track your location. We never sell, rent or trade any personal data.

4.Phone permissions and why they are used

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can refuse or withdraw any permission in Android Settings; only that feature will stop working.

  • Camera and photos: to add a labourer's photo or ID-card photo. Photos stay in the app's storage on your phone (and in your own Google Drive backup).
  • Location (optional): only if you choose to save a site location for a job. Location is never tracked in the background by us.
  • Notifications: for job, payment and backup reminders.
  • Fingerprint / PIN: to unlock the app. Your biometric data is handled by Android and is never seen or stored by us.

5.Google Sign-In and Google Drive

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with the app. For backups, the app asks for access only to the files it creates itself in your Google Drive (in a private app folder or a "Labour Diary Backups" folder). It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files. Backups go directly from your phone to your Drive; they do not pass through our servers.

Labour Diary's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not let people read it except with your permission, for security, or to comply with law.

Your use of Google services is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6.Why we use information

  • To provide the app features you use: records, bills, reports, reminders, sharing, backup and restore.
  • To create and manage your account and licence, run the free trial, and stop misuse of trials and licences.
  • In the Several phones mode, to sync data between your phones and give staff the access you choose.
  • To answer your support requests on phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To keep the service secure and to meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We process your information on the basis of your consent, which you give when you choose a mode, sign in and use the app, and for legitimate uses allowed by law. You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 10); doing so does not affect processing already done.

7.Sharing

  • When you share: slips, bills, cards, reports or exports that you send through WhatsApp or other apps go to whoever you choose. Please share carefully.
  • Service providers: Google (sign-in and your Drive backups) and the hosting company whose servers run our licence and sync service. They process data only to provide those services.
  • Legal reasons: to a court, government or law-enforcement authority when Indian law requires it.
  • Business transfer: if AKsys's business is transferred, this policy will continue to protect your data, and we will tell you in advance.

Nothing else. We do not sell personal data.

8.Security

We use reasonable security practices as required by Rule 8 of the IT (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011, including: an app lock with fingerprint, face or PIN and auto-lock; credentials kept in Android's secure storage; passwords stored only as salted hashes; encrypted HTTPS for every connection to our servers; signed licences; limited staff access; and daily server backups.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your phone locked, do not share your PIN or password, and keep backups switched on. If we learn of a data breach that affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

9.How long we keep data

  • Only this phone: data stays on your phone and your Drive until you delete it. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the phone; your Drive backups stay until you delete them from Google Drive. We keep only your licence record.
  • Several phones: we keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, or your subscription stays expired for more than 12 months, we delete your business data from the server within 30 days, and from server backups within a further 30 days.
  • Licence and payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting laws require (generally up to 8 years).

10.Your rights and choices

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and get a summary of it;
  • correct, complete or update it (most of it you can change yourself in the app);
  • ask us to delete your account and data;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • nominate a person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to; and
  • complain to us, and if not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

You can also export your data from the app at any time. To use any of these rights, contact us as shown in section 13. We may need to confirm that the request is from you.

11.Children

Labour Diary is a business app for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data of children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and for important changes we will also tell you in the app or on WhatsApp before they apply.
